# Take the most of the chosen stack; write your own only when there is no choice

We have a chosen stack. We take everything it gives, and we write nothing of our own if
the stack already does it. Our own code — only where there is no choice or where the ready
thing plainly does not fit.

**Why.** Our own code has to be written, checked, repaired and explained, and it is always
poorer than the ready one: the ready one has someone else's work and someone else's
mistakes behind it. Every thing of our own is one more place where we part with everyone
else's habits, and one more mechanism to hold in the head.

**How to apply.**

1. Before writing — look in the stack: a plugin, a ready field, a ready command, a ready
   table.
2. The ready thing is taken even if it is slightly wider than the task. Narrowing is a line
   of settings, an own implementation is constant work. That is how company keys are taken:
   `better-auth` runs them, not us.
3. The stack is chosen once. A second mechanism next to the first is never started: two
   truths for one job always drift apart.
4. Refusing the ready thing is a decision with a reason, not a default, and the reason is
   written down.

**Check:** if an own mechanism has appeared next to a ready one, it is a violation until a
reason is named. An example of a lawful refusal: our own MCP to Cloudflare — the ready one
reaches the platform with its own key past our filter, and there is no company boundary on
that path.
